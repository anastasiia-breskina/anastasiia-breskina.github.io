## About

Computer vision and machine learning researcher with active work in video-based behavioural analysis. Current research develops a privacy-aware multimodal framework for temporally-grounded behavioural analysis from video – modelling sequences of facial Action Units, gaze, and head pose over time rather than classifying isolated frames, with regulatory compliance (GDPR, EU AI Act) embedded in the architecture. Online assessment integrity serves as the primary validation domain; the methodology is designed for broader application across regulated environments.
